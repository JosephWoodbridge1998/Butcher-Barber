Deposit, cancellation & rescheduling policy

By booking an appointment with Butcherbarberuk, you agree to our policy that deposits are non-refundable in case of cancellation less than 2 hours in advance, late more than 10 minutes of your booking time, late without notice, or no-show. However, if you reschedule your appointment using the provided link, your deposit will automatically be transferred to your rescheduled reservation. Thank you for understanding and complying with our terms and conditions.
